# CREMA-D: four model utterances for Speak Model with Emotion

Four clips of one actor (1074) of CREMA-D, the sentence "It's eleven o'clock" (IEO) at the High level of four emotions. They are the ready-made model utterances of the App Speak Model with Emotion (CAE-SME, `Apps/CAE/SME`), and test data.

| File | Emotion | Offered as | Recognised from the voice alone (CREMA-D summaryTable.csv) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `1074_IEO_ANG_HI.wav` | Anger, High | Enraged | Anger, level 85.5 |
| `1074_IEO_SAD_HI.wav` | Sadness, High | Sad | Sad, level 64.71 |
| `1074_IEO_HAP_HI.wav` | Happy, High | Happy | Happy, level 78.0 |
| `1074_IEO_FEA_HI.wav` | Fear, High | Fearful | Fear, level 65.5 |

WAV, 16 kHz, mono, 16 bit, 1.7 to 2.6 s. Actor 1074 was chosen (2026-11-14) because the voice-only raters recognised all four emotions, with the highest mean level of the five actors for whom that is true.

Source: https://github.com/CheyneyComputerScience/CREMA-D, folder AudioWAV (also at https://media.githubusercontent.com/media/CheyneyComputerScience/CREMA-D/master/AudioWAV/<file>, which a browser may fetch).
