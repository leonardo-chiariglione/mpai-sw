PGM-AUA: the Levels of Basic Knowledge

The same sentence goes through the A-User three times. Level 0: A-User Control asks Basic Knowledge for no Communicative Function; A-User Formation moves the avatar from the speech only. Level 1: Basic Knowledge gives functions (what the body should mean, towards whom, on which words); Behaviour Planning in A-User Formation chooses the Behaviours and times them on the words. Level 2: Basic Knowledge also chooses the Behaviour of each function; A-User Formation realises it or reports why not. Press Play to see the avatar say it.
