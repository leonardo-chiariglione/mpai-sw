THALIA - for the participants of the MPAI meetings in the MMM world
=====================================================================

Thalia is the MPAI environment. With it you:
  - enter the MMM world, where the MPAI meetings are held (your MMM User), and
  - use the Apps of MPAI as a Service (they run on MPAI's machine; the microphone, the camera and the screen are yours).

What you need: a Windows PC (64 bit) with Microsoft Edge, and a headset (no loudspeakers: the meeting room has its own echo).

ONCE
1. Install Tailscale (https://tailscale.com/download) and sign in (with Google, Microsoft, GitHub... or an email).
   Accept the invitation to MPAI's machine that you received from Tailscale (open its link while signed in).
   MPAI's machine is reached through Tailscale only: nothing of it is on the public internet.
2. Unzip this folder anywhere (for example on the Desktop) and double-click Thalia.bat.
   (If Windows says it protected your PC, choose "More info", then "Run anyway".)
3. In Thalia, Home, "The MMM world":
   - Account Level: Participant (your Persona stays the same from one meeting to the next)
   - your first name, last name, organisation, and THE EMAIL ADDRESS YOUR INVITATION WAS SENT TO
     (the invitation to the meeting is for the Account with that email)
   - a password of at least 6 characters (Thalia keeps it, encrypted, on this PC)
   Press Register. The world opens in its own window: choose your Persona (your avatar). This takes a while, the first time only.
   Allow the microphone when Edge asks.

EVERY TIME
Double-click Thalia.bat: Thalia enters the MMM world by itself (Tailscale must be on). Nothing to type, nothing downloaded again.
Click the point you want to reach: your Persona walks there - through the MPAI doors, across the atrium to the south wing;
the MMM meeting room is the first on the right, and you sit down by yourself. The mouse wheel turns you; Ctrl + wheel zooms; Q stands up, E sits.

The world and the Apps answer while MPAI's machine offers them (during the meetings).
