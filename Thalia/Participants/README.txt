THALIA - for the participants of the MPAI meetings in the MMM world
=====================================================================

Thalia is the MPAI environment. With it you:
  - enter the MMM world, where the MPAI meetings are held (your MMM User), and
  - use the Apps of MPAI as a Service (they run on MPAI's machine; the microphone, the camera and the screen are yours).

What you need: a Windows PC (64 bit) with Microsoft Edge, a headset (no loudspeakers: the meeting room has its own echo). Nothing to install.

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (for example on the Desktop) and double-click Thalia.bat.
   If Windows says it protected your PC, choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
2. In Thalia, Home, "The MMM world" (or Settings, "Your MMM User"):
   - Account Level: Participant (your Persona stays the same from one meeting to the next)
   - your first name, last name, organisation, and THE EMAIL ADDRESS YOUR INVITATION WAS SENT TO
     (the invitation is for the Account with that email)
   - a password of at least 6 characters (Thalia keeps it, encrypted, on this PC)
   Press Register once. Afterwards, at each meeting, press "Enter the MMM": the world opens, with nothing to type.
3. The first time, choose your Persona (your avatar) in the world. Allow the microphone when Edge asks.
   Click the point you want to reach: your Persona walks there - through the MPAI doors, across the atrium to the south wing;
   the MMM meeting room is the first on the right, and you sit down by yourself. The mouse wheel turns you; Ctrl + wheel zooms; Q stands up, E sits.

"Enter the MMM" works while the meeting is on; before, it says so.
The Apps of MPAI as a Service are in Thalia's Apps; they work while MPAI's Service is on.
